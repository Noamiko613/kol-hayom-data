siman 1: law 1: When a person recites the Shema in the evening, they should recite it after the time when the priests enter to eat their terumah, which is when the stars appear.  
 law 2: The time for reciting the evening Shema extends until midnight, according to the majority of opinions. However, if one recites it after midnight, they still fulfill their obligation, but without the blessing.  
 siman 2: law 1: In the morning, the time for reciting Shema begins when one can distinguish between blue and white threads.  
 law 2: The ideal time for reciting the morning Shema is until the third hour of the day, which is approximately one quarter of the daylight hours.  
 siman 3: law 1: When reciting Shema, one should have proper kavanah (intention) and not be distracted by other thoughts.  
 law 2: It is customary to cover one's eyes with the hand when reciting the first verse to help concentrate.  
 siman 4: law 1: The blessings before and after Shema should be recited with the same level of attention and reverence.  
 law 2: One should stand during the recitation of the Amidah prayer that follows Shema.  
 siman 5: law 1: On weekdays, the morning service includes the recitation of Psalms and other prayers before the Amidah.  
 law 2: The order of prayers may vary slightly depending on the community's custom, but the core elements remain constant.
