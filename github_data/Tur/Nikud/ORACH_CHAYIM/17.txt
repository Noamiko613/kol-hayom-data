17. A blind man is obligated for tzitzit, but women and slaves are exempt. Hermaphrodites and androgynous persons are obligated out of doubt. Rambam wrote: “They wrap themselves without a blessing.” He goes on, according to his method, explaining that women cannot recite a blessing over something for which they are exempt. But Rabbenu Tam wrote that they may recite a blessing, even though they are exempt, but it is better that they not recite a blessing. A minor who knows how to wrap himself: His father is required to buy tzitzit for him, to teach him.
